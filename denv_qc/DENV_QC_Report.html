Insert Size Distribution — Concern #1

SerotypePeak InsertRange
DENV-167 bp38 - 87 bp
DENV-249 bp39 - 88 bp
DENV-369 bp48 - 87 bp
Issue: Insert peaks at 38–87bp are far shorter than the 2x231bp read length. A 50bp insert sequenced at 2x231bp means each read extends ~180bp past the fragment into adapter.

Why Inserts Are So Short

  • Degraded clinical RNA — plasma viral RNA is often highly fragmented
  • PCR amplicon protocol — may use short tiling amplicons by design
  • Over-fragmentation during library prep

Consequences

  • R1 & R2 overlap extensively → reads are largely redundant
  • Massive adapter read-through → explains ~15–20% read loss during trimming
  • Overlap enables error correction (applied by fastp)
  • Effective unique sequence per pair is only ~50–87bp
Mitigated: fastp adapter trimming + overlap-based error correction recover usable data. Short inserts reduce effective throughput but do not compromise consensus quality given the sequencing depth.

Key evidence this is a run-level instrument artifact:
  • Affects all 40 samples identically
  • R1 is completely clean at the same positions
  • Each spike is exactly 1 cycle wide
  • Quality recovers over 3–4 cycles post-spike
R2 GC content also shows dips at positions 24, 59, 82, 131 — because N-called bases are excluded from GC calculation, distorting composition at those cycles.

R2 Artifact — Diagnosis & Impact

Most Likely Cause

During Read 2 sequencing, the MiSeq experienced imaging failures at specific cycles — most likely caused by:

  • Air bubbles passing through the flowcell during those cycles
  • Reagent delivery failures at those cycles
  • Focus loss during imaging

The irregular spacing between spikes (35, 23, 49 cycles apart) rules out a periodic mechanical cause and points to stochastic bubble/reagent events.

Summary & Next Steps

QC Verdict

  • 40/40 samples pass QC
  • Consistently high quality (Q30 > 94%)
  • Adequate depth for all samples
  • Low duplication (median 10.2%)
  • GC content consistent with DENV
Two quality concerns identified:
  1. Short insert sizes (38–87bp peak) — degraded clinical RNA; mitigated by overlap correction
  2. R2 imaging failures at cycles 25, 60, 83, 132 — run-level MiSeq artifact; cleaned by fastp N-filtering
Neither issue compromises downstream analysis.
